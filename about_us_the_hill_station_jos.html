EVOLUTION

From 1938 to a New Chapter of Plateau Hospitality

The story of The Hill Station began in 1938, and over the decades, its corridors welcomed remarkable guests and witnessed defining moments in history. In 1956, Queen Elizabeth II visited The Hill Station during her journey through Nigeria. Years later, political leaders, military officers, and influential figures would also pass through its doors, adding new chapters to a story that continues to unfold.

Today, we honor that legacy through thoughtful restoration and modern innovation. Preserving the character of our iconic stone architecture while embracing contemporary design, sustainable energy systems, wellness facilities, and elevated guest experiences, we have reimagined The Hill Station for a new generation.

The spirit remains.
Only experience has evolved.

OUR NARRATIVE

Where Heritage Meets the Highlands

Nestled within the cool highlands of Jos, The Hill Station is a contemporary luxury destination with a story unlike any other. For nearly a century, its distinctive stone architecture has stood as a landmark of hospitality, welcoming travelers, dignitaries, explorers, and generations of families to Plateau.

Originally established as one of the region's most celebrated European hotels, The Hill Station became a sanctuary for those drawn to the temperate climate and remarkable landscapes of Jos. Today, that legacy lives on through elegantly appointed rooms and suites, curated dining experiences including The Laundry Café and The Skyview Lounge, serene wellness amenities, and thoughtfully designed spaces for business, celebration, and connection.

OUR COMMITMENT

Grounded in the Plateau

For nearly a century, The Hill Station has been inseparable from the story of Plateau State. The landscapes, communities, and cultures that surround us have shaped who we are, and we believe true hospitality means giving back to the place we call home.

  • LOCAL SOURCING

    Our culinary philosophy begins in the fertile soils of the Jos highlands. By partnering with local farmers and producers, we celebrate regional ingredients while supporting sustainable livelihoods within our community.

  • CULTURAL PRESERVATION

    The rich traditions of Plateau State continue to inspire our spaces and experiences. Through collaborations with local artisans and craftspeople, we help preserve cultural heritage while sharing its beauty with guests from around the world.

  • RESPONSIBLE STEWARDSHIP

    From water conservation initiatives to environmentally conscious operations, we are committed to protecting the natural resources that have made Plateau a destination of distinction for generations.
