Resort Guidelines

Ensuring a serene and respectful environment for all our guests at The Hill Station.

Check-in from 15:00. Check-out by 11:00. Early arrivals are subject to availability.

Smoke-Free Environment

For the health and comfort of our guests, the entire resort is strictly non-smoking.

While we love animals, The Hill Station remains a pet-free sanctuary to preserve our heritage interiors.
