DINING & LOUNGES

Curated Dining

From The Laundry Café to The Skyview Lounge, The Grand Terrace, and The Pool Bar, dining at The Hill Station is thoughtfully crafted around flavor, atmosphere, and the timeless art of gathering.

01 / LAUNDRY CAFÉ

The Laundry Café

Begin your day where the aroma of freshly baked sourdough fills the air and every cup is rich with heritage and crafted with care. The Laundry Café offers a welcoming retreat for slow mornings, casual meetings, and moments of quiet indulgence.

02 / SKYVIEW LOUNGE

The Skyview Lounge

Perched at the highest point of The Hill Station, The Skyview Lounge offers one of Jos's most captivating vantage points. With sweeping 270-degree views of Plateau, it is a place where conversations linger a little longer, and every sunset feels like a private performance. Whether enjoying handcrafted cocktails, light bites, or simply the cool highland breeze, The Skyview Lounge invites guests to experience Jos from a different perspective.

03 / POOL BAR

The Pool Bar

A relaxed and inviting setting designed for effortless connection. From freshly pressed juices and light refreshments to curated wines, premium spirits, and signature cocktails, The Pool Bar provides the perfect backdrop for casual gatherings, post-meeting conversations, and unhurried evenings. Comfortable, elegant, and distinctly The Hill Station.

04 / RESTAURANT

The Grand Terrace

Rooted in the spirit of hospitality that has defined The Hill Station for generations, The Grand Terrace offers a refined dining experience shaped by fresh ingredients, attentive service, and the relaxed sophistication of Plateau. Whether sharing a celebratory meal, hosting a business lunch, or enjoying an intimate evening, every table is set for meaningful moments.
