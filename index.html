OUR HERITAGE

Welcome to
The Hill Station

For nearly a century, The Hill Station has stood as one of Plateau’s most recognisable hospitality landmarks. Its stone architecture, cool climate, and storied guest history have made it a place of arrival for travellers, dignitaries, families, and communities across generations. Today, The Hill Station returns renewed: refined, restored, and reimagined for modern stays, corporate gatherings, leisure, dining, wellness, and discovery.

HIGHLAND WAY OF LIFE

Calm, Comfort, and the Quiet Beauty of the Plateau

Life at The Hill Station moves at a more considered pace. Slow mornings. Fresh air. Warm interiors. Thoughtful service. Long conversations over good food. Views that remind you exactly where you are. Every part of the resort is shaped to help guests feel restored, welcomed, and quietly looked after.

Rooms and suites designed for comfort, privacy, warmth, and ease.

Dining, lounges, terraces, meeting rooms, and event spaces for moments with meaning.

Resort walks, highland experiences, cultural connection, and access to the best of Jos.
