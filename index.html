OUR HERITAGE

Welcome to
The Hill Station

For nearly a century, The Hill Station has stood as one of Plateau’s most recognisable hospitality landmark. Its stone architecture, cool climate, and storied guest history have made it a place of arrival for travellers, dignitaries, families, and communities across generations. Today, The Hill Station returns renewed: refined, restored, and reimagined for modern stays, corporate gatherings, leisure, dining, wellness, and discovery.
