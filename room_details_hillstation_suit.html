Room Overview

A Stay with Room to Breathe

The Hill Station Suites are designed for guests who value both comfort and space. Featuring a dedicated living area, dining corner, and thoughtfully arranged interiors, the suite provides a seamless balance between relaxation and functionality.

Whether staying for a weekend escape, an extended business visit, or a special occasion, the suite offers the comfort of a private residence with the service and attention of a luxury hotel.
