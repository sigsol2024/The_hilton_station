## Brand & Style

The brand personality is **Elevated, Refined, and Timeless**, drawing deep inspiration from the natural heritage of Jos. The visual identity avoids the clinical nature of modern tech, instead opting for an **Editorial Minimalism** that feels like a high-end travel journal. 

The design narrative focuses on "The Luxury of Space." By utilizing generous margins and an asymmetrical layout, the system creates a sense of calm and exclusivity. It evokes the feeling of a mountain retreat—cool, quiet, and grounded. Every interaction is intentional, moving away from high-frequency "engagement" patterns toward a slow, immersive experience.

Visual hallmarks include:
- **Natural Authenticity:** Using organic textures and earth-toned colors.
- **Heritage Modernism:** Blending traditional luxury serif typography with a structured, airy layout.
- **Architectural Influence:** Subtle geometric motifs derived from local rock formations and the "Hill Station" peak logo.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in the landscape of the plateau. **Deep Forest Green** serves as the primary anchor, used for headers, primary backgrounds, and deep brand moments to signify heritage and luxury. **Warm Sand** replaces pure white as the primary surface color, providing a softer, more sophisticated background that feels tactile and welcoming.

**Muted Earth Gold** is used exclusively for calls to action and premium highlights; it is a matte, earthy pigment rather than a shimmering metallic. **Soft Sky Blue** is used as a functional "air" color—appearing in thin dividers, secondary icon backgrounds, or subtle image overlays to provide a cool contrast to the warmer tones.

- **Primary:** #1B3022 (Deep Forest Green)
- **Secondary (Surface):** #F2E8D5 (Warm Sand)
- **Accent:** #C5A059 (Earth Gold)
- **Support:** #D1E3E7 (Sky Blue)
- **Text:** Primary Green is used for headings; #2D2926 is used for body copy.

## Typography

Typography follows an editorial hierarchy. **Libre Caslon Text** provides the authoritative, literary voice required for a heritage property. It should be used with tight tracking for large display headers to emphasize the elegance of its serifs.

**Hanken Grotesk** is the functional counterpart. It is a modern sans-serif with a neutral but warm character. Body copy should maintain a generous line height (1.6) to ensure the reading experience feels unhurried. Use **label-caps** for navigation items and small sub-headers to provide a structured, organized feel against the more fluid serif headings.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy is **Asymmetrical Editorial**. Rather than a rigid, repeating grid, elements should feel placed with intention. 

- **Fluid 12-Column Grid:** Used as a base, but elements frequently break the grid or span unusual column counts (e.g., a 5-column image offset against 7 columns of whitespace).
- **Layering:** Images and text blocks should occasionally overlap by small increments (24-48px) to create visual depth, mimicking a physical scrapbook or high-end magazine.
- **Generous Voids:** Use large vertical gaps (`section-gap`) between content blocks to allow the design to "breathe" and signal a transition in the narrative.
- **Mobile Reflow:** On mobile, the asymmetry collapses into a single-column stacked layout, but maintains "breathing room" through 24px side margins and increased vertical padding between cards.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy shadows and floating SaaS-style cards. Depth is conveyed through **Tonal Layering** and **Subtle Parallax**:

- **Layered Surfaces:** Use the Primary Green on top of Warm Sand backgrounds to create a clear sense of what is "on top."
- **Low-Contrast Outlines:** Instead of shadows, use 1px solid borders in a slightly darker shade of the background (e.g., a darker tan border on a Warm Sand background) to define interactive zones.
- **Soft Parallax:** Image containers should have a subtle scroll-speed difference compared to text to create a physical sense of "looking through" windows into the property.
- **Backdrop Blurs:** Sparingly used for navigation overlays, using a heavily blurred version of the background colors to maintain the "Glassmorphism" feel but in a warmer, organic context.

## Shapes

The shape language is primarily **Soft and Geometric**.
- **Standard Corners:** A subtle 0.25rem (4px) radius is applied to buttons and small UI elements to take the "edge" off without making them feel bubbly or informal.
- **The Peak Motif:** Use a subtle 45-degree angled "corner cut" or a mountain-top graphic divider for large section transitions, referencing the Jos rock formations.
- **Image Frames:** Large hero images should remain sharp-cornered (0px) to maintain a professional, architectural photography aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Muted Earth Gold background with Deep Forest Green text. No shadow, 1px inset border for a "pressed" look on hover.
- **Secondary:** Deep Forest Green background with Warm Sand text. 
- **Ghost:** Transparent background with 1px Deep Forest Green border.

### Input Fields
- Underline style only. A 1px Deep Forest Green line under the text, with the label in `label-caps` floating above. No box containers for inputs to keep the layout feeling open.

### Cards
- No shadows. Cards are defined by a background color change (e.g., a Warm Sand card on a slightly darker tan section) or a thin 1px border. 
- Images within cards should have a subtle zoom-in effect on hover.

### Icons
- **Hospitality Set:** Custom 1px thin-stroke icons. Icons should be oversized and centered within Sky Blue circular or Peak-shaped containers.

### Navigation
- A centered, minimalist top-bar. Links use the `label-caps` style. On scroll, the background transitions from transparent to a solid Warm Sand with a soft bottom border.
